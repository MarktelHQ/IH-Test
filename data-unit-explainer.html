🧩So what is DATA UNIT's job?

That whole chain above is run by one big piece of software called an ERP system. DATA UNIT doesn't invent it — a giant German company called SAP does. DATA UNIT are the people who install it, bend it into the shape a particular business needs, and keep it running afterwards.

Everyone keeps their own list

  • 📊 Sales writes orders in Excel. The warehouse keeps a paper folder. Accounting has a third list.
  • 😬 Somebody sells 200 screws that were already promised to another customer yesterday.
  • 📞 A customer phones asking where their delivery is. Four people get asked. Nobody knows.
  • 🧮 At the end of the month, working out whether the company actually made money takes a week.
  • 🗺️ Every new employee has to learn where all the secret lists live.

🪑Who's sitting at which desk

Roughly 50 people work at DATA UNIT. Almost all of them do one of these five jobs. Worth spotting which one looks most fun to you — that's a great thing to say at the end of the day.

🧭

Consultant

Beraterin / Berater

Sits with the customer and asks a hundred questions about how their business actually works, then decides how the software should be set up to match. Half detective, half translator.

Their day: meetings, whiteboards, writing down what a company does step by step.

⌨️

Developer

Entwickler / Programmiererin

Writes the code for the bits SAP doesn't do out of the box. A Swiss company might need a special label printed, or their webshop wired into the warehouse. That's a developer's job.

Their day: code, testing, fixing the thing that broke at 4pm.

🗓️

Project manager

Projektleiterin / Projektleiter

Switching a company onto a new ERP takes months and can't go wrong — the business has to keep selling the whole time. This person keeps the plan, the budget and everyone's nerves in one piece.

Their day: plans, deadlines, chasing people, calming customers down.

🎧

Support

Support

When something stops working at a customer, they phone here. Support takes the ticket, works out what's wrong, and fixes it — often while the customer is still on the line.

Their day: tickets, phone calls, detective work, quick wins.

🤝

Sales

Verkauf

Finds companies that need a new system and convinces them DATA UNIT is the right partner. Has to understand the technology well enough to be honest about what it can't do.

Their day: customer visits, demos, offers, lots of driving.
