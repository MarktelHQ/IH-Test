    What would settle it

    Every amber finding resolves one way or the other within two or three quarters. Next results are due late November 2026.

    1. Cash conversion back above 70% of profitThis quarter it was 40%. Two more quarters below 60% and the revenue is a financing programme rather than a sales result.
    2. Collection time back below 55 daysIt moved from 45 to 60 in one quarter. A further rise means longer terms are the price of the next order, not a one-off.
    3. Gross margin bottoms near 71% and recoversGuidance is 74%, down from 75%. A sustained slide below 70% breaks the pricing-power case.
    4. Independent demand grows faster than the guaranteesThe one that matters most. If guarantees, stakes and extended terms keep growing in line with revenue, the ecosystem is not yet self-funding.
    5. Goodwill survives its first impairment review$21.1bn, quadrupled in two years by a deal struck near a cycle peak.
