What you're not paying for

Most of what makes traditional Swiss financial advice expensive has nothing to do with the advice. Here's everything you're not buying.
  • ✕Commission-driven product selling. Providers are ranked on total cost alone, and any commercial relationship we have is disclosed on the recommendation itself.
  • ✕An insurance policy dressed up as an investment. We don't sell insurance and we aren't licensed to.
  • ✕A percentage of your assets. Your fee doesn't grow just because your portfolio did.
  • ✕Half a day off work for a meeting. Everything happens online, in your own time.
  • ✕A 60-page plan you read once. Short written recommendations you can actually act on.
  • ✕A relationship manager who calls you. No cold calls, no upsells, no quarterly pitch.
  • ✕The Porsche in the car park. No marble reception, no sponsored golf day, no company 911. We're a small digital business and the value goes to you, not the overheads.
Also worth knowing before you buy: advice is written rather than by phone, so there's always a record of it. Questions are answered in your next review rather than between them. We hold no access to your accounts, so only you can move money. And we don't advise on crypto, mortgages, insurance or your occupational pension, because those sit outside what this registration covers and we would rather say so than guess.

Why you can trust the answer

Independence is easy to claim and hard to prove. Here's how it's actually built.

Independent ranking

Providers are ranked by total cost alone, by a rule, before any human looks at it. Where we have a commercial relationship with a provider that appears in your recommendation, it is disclosed on the recommendation itself, in the same place every time, and never buried in a footer.

Every number carries its source and its date

Not "roughly 0.4%". The exact figure, where it was read and when. If a provider reprices, our data updates once and every affected member is flagged automatically.

A named, qualified person is accountable

Advice is given by a client adviser registered under Art. 6 FinSA, affiliated to an ombudsman and covered by professional indemnity insurance. Nothing reaches you without being read and approved by that person.

Nothing is written by a machine on its own

Software finds what changed and drafts the explanation. It never decides what to recommend, and it never sees your name or your email. A qualified human decides, edits and signs.

When you should not buy this

We would rather say it here than take your money and tell you in your first review.

  • You have expensive debt. Paying off a card at 9% beats anything we could recommend. Clear it, then come back.
  • You have no emergency fund yet. Part 2 of the course covers this properly, and it matters more than optimising a 3a.
  • Your total invested is small. Below roughly CHF 30,000 the fee differences we would find are unlikely to cover what this costs. Keep contributing and revisit.
  • You already hold the cheapest products and your allocation matches your profile. Some of you will. If your opening review says exactly that, cancel before renewal. You'll have paid CHF 548 to have a qualified person confirm you were right, which is a legitimate thing to buy once.

Questions

The ones people actually ask.

How is this different from the course, or from your free articles?

Those are education. They explain how things work and they have to speak to everybody, so they can never tell you what you personally should do. This is regulated personal advice: a qualified adviser assesses your circumstances, reaches a conclusion about your accounts specifically, and puts it in writing with their name and register number on it. Different product, different rules, different person.

What does "qualified" and "regulated" actually mean here?

Advice is given by a client adviser registered under Art. 6 of the Swiss Financial Services Act. That brings a formal suitability assessment, a documented record of every recommendation and the data behind it, mandatory conflict of interest disclosure, affiliation with a recognised ombudsman and professional indemnity insurance. Plenty of people selling financial products in Switzerland aren't held to that.

Do you connect to my bank or see my accounts?

No, and we don't want to. You enter your balances yourself, roughly once a quarter, and it takes about two minutes. We hold no login, no authority and no ability to move a single franc. That's a design decision, not a limitation we're working around.

What if my review says nothing needs changing?

Then it says so, and it lists everything that was checked and found correct. For a well-organised member that will be the answer in most quarters, and it's worth knowing. If it is the answer every quarter for a year, cancel. We won't be offended.

Do you earn commission from the providers you recommend?

Investing Hero has referral arrangements with some providers and we publish that on every page. Inside this service the ranking rule sorts on total cost alone and can't see which providers pay us, so a provider can't buy its way up your list. Where a recommendation names a provider we have a relationship with, that's stated on the recommendation itself.

Can I speak to someone?

Not in the first year. Everything is written, which means there's always a record of exactly what was advised and what it was based on, and it means we can keep the price where it's. You get one written question per quarter, answered inside your review. If a call option is added later it will be priced separately.

What happens to my data and my advice file?

Your file holds your details, your assessments, every recommendation and the data each one was based on. You can download all of it at any time. It's retained for ten years, including after you cancel, because we're required to keep it. Your name and email are never sent to any AI system.

I am leaving Switzerland. Is this for me?

The subscription is for Swiss residents. If you're planning to leave, the decisions that matter are different ones and there's a separate one-off Leaving Switzerland review for exactly that. If you leave while subscribed, tell us and we'll handle it properly rather than carry on as though nothing changed.

Can I cancel?

Any time before renewal. Your advice file stays available to download, and your course and knowledge base access are unaffected because those came with the course.
