Why

In plain terms

Your 3a money is in a bank fund charging 1.05% a year, all in. It holds broadly the same global equities as the cheapest fund-based 3a products in Switzerland, which charge between 0.39% and 0.44%. You are paying roughly two and a half times the going rate for the same market exposure.

Your risk answers put you comfortably in a high-equity strategy, and your current fund is already 94% equities, so this is a change of provider rather than a change of risk. Nothing about your exposure to markets changes.

There is no exit charge on your current product and no minimum holding period, so there is nothing to weigh against the saving.
